Add Orca to your Home Screen

Install Orca Orca Web as an app: it launches fullscreen from its own icon, works offline, and feels native. No app store needed — it’s a Progressive Web App.

Just open the app →

🤖 Android · Chrome

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm with Install — the Orca icon appears on your home screen.

🍎 iPhone / iPad · Safari

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⎋↑ (bottom bar).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — look for the Orca icon.

💻 Desktop · Chrome / Edge

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon (⊕ in a monitor) at the right of the address bar — or use the Install button above when visible.
  3. Click Install. Orca opens in its own window and joins your apps.

Why install?

  • Fullscreen, standalone window — no browser chrome, just Orca.
  • Offline capable — the app shell and your saved pages work without a network.
  • Faster launch — the shell is cached by a service worker; it opens instantly.
  • Home-screen shortcuts — long-press the icon for “New tab” and “New incognito tab”.
  • Your data stays put — bookmarks, history and passwords are stored on-device.

Having trouble? Make sure you’re on HTTPS (Vercel does this automatically) and that you’ve opened the site at least once.
